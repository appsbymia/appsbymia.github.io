Privacy Policy for Inkle

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Data Collection

Inkle is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, reading history, or vocabulary notes to external servers.

2. iCloud Storage

Your study notes and backup files are stored locally on your device and synced via your personal Apple iCloud account. We have no access to your iCloud data.

3. System Translation & Dictionary

Text translation and dictionary lookups rely on iOS system services and local offline dictionaries. No text content is tracked by us.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please send an email to appsbymia@gmail.com.
