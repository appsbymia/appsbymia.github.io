Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Inkle (the “App”).

Inkle is an English EPUB reading, listening, translation, and review tool for Chinese-speaking learners. The App is designed to work locally on your device. No account or registration is required, and Inkle does not operate an external server for your books or learning data.

1. Information Stored by the App

The EPUB books you import, your reading position, and the learning content you create—such as saved words, phrases, sentences, highlight colors, and notes—are stored locally on your device. Inkle does not upload your EPUB files, book text, reading activity, or learning content to a server operated by the Developer.

The built-in ECDICT dictionary is packaged with the App as an offline SQLite database. Looking up a word uses this local dictionary and does not require an Inkle account or an Inkle server.

The App does not require or create a user account and does not collect:

  • Your account credentials
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Your imported EPUB files or book content
  • Your saved learning content or reading history

2. Translation and Text-to-Speech

Inkle provides English-to-Chinese word definitions and translation features. Word definitions use the offline ECDICT dictionary. Text translation and text-to-speech reading use Apple system services available on your device. Inkle does not operate its own translation or text-to-speech server and does not receive or store translation or speech data on an Inkle server.

The availability and handling of information by Apple system services may depend on your device, system settings, network connection, and Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

3. Local Storage and EPUB Files

Imported EPUB files and App data are stored on your device. Inkle does not access your general Files library beyond the files you explicitly choose to import through Apple's system file picker. EPUB files that require publisher authorization or use other technical restrictions may not be importable.

If you export or share an EPUB or learning-book file, its later storage and handling depend on the destination you choose and are outside Inkle's control. Deleting the App generally removes its local data, but does not necessarily delete files you exported or data already stored in iCloud Drive.

4. Learning Book Backup and iCloud

If you enable the App's iCloud backup, Inkle stores your learning book in your iCloud Drive, in the Inkle folder. The backup contains your saved learning content, including saved items, highlight colors, and notes. It does not contain your imported EPUB books or your review history. Review sessions are generated locally and are not stored as review records.

iCloud transmission and storage are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. The Developer does not operate your iCloud account and does not have access to your iCloud Drive files.

You can remove the backup file from the Files app by opening iCloud Drive > Inkle and deleting studybook.json. You can also remove individual saved items from inside the App. Deleting a file from iCloud Drive may affect the availability of the learning-book backup on your devices.

5. Notifications

Daily reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. Inkle does not use a remote push-notification server for these reminders and does not send your reminder schedule to an Inkle server.

6. Purchases

Inkle may offer Inkle Pro as a one-time, non-subscription in-app purchase. Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The Developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process transaction and Apple Account information under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Apple Diagnostics

Inkle does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. Depending on your device analytics settings, Apple may process and provide the Developer with limited technical diagnostics, such as crash information. This is handled by Apple's system services and is subject to Apple's privacy practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Inkle is not specifically directed to children. Because the App does not create accounts or transmit App content to the Developer, the Developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App.

9. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include so that we can respond. Please do not send your EPUB files, learning-book backup, or other private learning content unless we specifically ask for it and you are comfortable doing so.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App or its data-handling practices change. The latest version will show its effective date above.
