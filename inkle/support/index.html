App Support

Thank you for using Inkle.

Getting Started

From the bookshelf, tap + and choose an .epub file from the Files app. Inkle stores imported books locally on your device. EPUB files that require publisher authorization or use other technical restrictions may not be importable.

Reading and Listening

  • Listen to English books with Apple's system text-to-speech voice, with the current sentence highlighted in real time.
  • Choose a sentence range and repeat it with continuous A-B looping or chapter looping for focused listening practice.
  • Continue playback in the background or from the Lock Screen.
  • Tap a word to see Chinese definitions from the offline ECDICT dictionary, including multiple definitions, and optionally save it.
  • Drag to select a word, phrase, part of a sentence, or a complete sentence for automatic translation.
  • View the system translation of an English paragraph.

Saved Content and Review

  • Save words, phrases, and sentences from a book. Saved content is highlighted in the book with one of seven selectable colors and linked to its original sentence.
  • Browse all saved content in the Learning Book, change highlight colors, add notes, or remove saved items.
  • In Learning Mode, review random or sequential cards, listen to the pronunciation, view Chinese definitions and the original sentence in context, and keep “Remembered” and “Not remembered” counts.
  • Use the dictation feature in Learning Mode to practice listening and check your writing.
  • Import or export your Learning Book and back it up with iCloud.

Other Features

  • Read Chinese EPUB files with the basic reading experience. Chinese EPUB files do not support listening or saved learning content.
  • Schedule a daily reminder using local notifications on your device.

Free Version and Inkle Pro

The free version supports one English book with limited trial content. Inkle Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited English books, full-book listening, and other Pro features. Chinese EPUB files support basic reading without the listening and learning-book features.

iCloud Learning-Book Backup

iCloud backup contains your saved learning content, highlight colors, and notes. It does not contain your imported EPUB books or review history. To remove the backup, open the Files app and delete iCloud Drive > Inkle > studybook.json. You can also remove saved items individually inside the App.

Restore a Purchase

Use the “Restore Purchase” option in the App and make sure you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
